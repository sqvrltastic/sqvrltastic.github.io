Welcome

Hi, I'm sqvrltastic and I'm a digital artist. This is my main place for posting my artworks and sharing my thoughts. Welcome to my website! 🎨

This website is part of the "Web Revival" movement, also known as "Small Web" or "Indie Web". I made it because I wanted to have a place to post my artwork away from the whims of social media. If you want to know more, you can check this page.

The content on this site is SFW, but it is not intended for children. Some artworks are slightly suggestive. Also, I swear, a lot occasionally. Keep that in mind.

If you want to reach me, your best bet is to shoot me an email at sqvrltastic(at)gmail.com; I check my email inbox regularly.

You can also sign my guestbook!

I also have a profile on GitHub and I post my art on Reddit, but I'm not as active there as I'm trying to limit my use of social media.

Commission status: closed.

I don't take commissions at the moment because, honestly, I just don't feel ready yet. For now, I am still focused on growing and developing a semi-consistent and efficient drawing process.

This website does not use any tracking scripts, fingerprinting, analytics, cookies, or any such nonsense!

Updates

You can subscribe to the website's RSS feed to receive news and updates when I post, no email or signup necessary. If you don't know what RSS is, I wrote an explanation here. Keep in mind though, my interests and activity levels change so please don't expect regular updates for any particular theme or fandom. I draw whatever catches my interest at the moment.
