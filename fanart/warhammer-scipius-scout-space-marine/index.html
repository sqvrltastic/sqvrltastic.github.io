Scipius as a Scout Space Marine

Published: 10/26/2026

View full (.jpg)

I hate Phobos-pattern armor, so I decided to draw my favorite snarky sniper as a space marine scout, because that's the superior version of the "lighter-armored space marine" concept and I will die on that hill.

I tried going for the old-school codex art style, to the best of my current ability...

Some WIP shots:

  • step one
  • step two

  • step four (I used an in-game screenshot I took to quickly sketch out the background)

  • adding more details, cleaning up, adding finishing touches and adding paper and noise texture

The background is based on "Bridge" map from the Space Marine 2 PvP mode.
